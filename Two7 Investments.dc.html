What We Do

Two7 Investments is Emory's first stock pitch oriented club. Chartered under Emory College, we strive to give students of every major and background the resources they need to succeed in stock pitching. Under our strict open-access policy, we provide education, community, and technical resources to any Emory student seeking it.

♠

Stock Pitch Excellence

Our technical focus is entirely on stock pitching. No fluff, no bureaucracy. Just the fundamental analysis and the skills to present convincing, professional investment ideas.

♥

Open to All

Every Emory student is welcome. Come to our semester education sessions, reach out to a current club member, or come to an event to learn more about finance or to find a competition team.

♦

Compete Nationally

We send teams to premier stock pitch competitions across the country. In our first semester, our members have gone head-to-head with top programs and consistently delivered.

♣

A Professional and Social Network

At Two7, we believe there is enough give-and-take in industry, that we don't need to create more of it in college. All of our members are intelligent, welcoming, and not out to get you.
